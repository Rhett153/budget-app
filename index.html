YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Privacy & backups

This vault is encrypted locally; cloud sync is optional and separately configured.

⌁

Account and encrypted sync

Set up a passkey account to open the same encrypted vault on your phone and computer. Your passkey signs you in; your vault passphrase encrypts your data. On each new device, enter the vault passphrase once, then choose to remember that device.

Cloud sync needs a private Supabase project configured for this app. No data is sent until you enable sync.

Cloud account is not connected.

◆

Local encrypted vault

Transactions, plan data, goals, and saved statement files are encrypted with AES-GCM before being stored in IndexedDB. The passphrase is not saved or sent anywhere. With sync enabled, the cloud receives only the encrypted vault record.

If you clear this browser’s site data, change devices, or forget the passphrase, you will need your encrypted backup to restore the vault.

⇩

Encrypted backup

Download an encrypted copy of your vault and keep it somewhere private. The backup still requires your passphrase.

⌑

Change passphrase

Re-encrypt the vault with a new passphrase. Use at least 12 characters.

×

Delete this local vault

Delete the encrypted vault from this browser. This cannot be undone unless you have an encrypted backup. Deleting it here does not remove a cloud copy.
