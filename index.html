YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

Privacy & backups

This vault exists only in this browser on this device.

◆

Local encrypted vault

Transactions, plan data, goals, and saved statement files are encrypted with AES-GCM before being stored in IndexedDB. The passphrase is not saved or sent anywhere.

If you clear this browser’s site data, change devices, or forget the passphrase, you will need your encrypted backup to restore the vault.

⇩

Encrypted backup

Download an encrypted copy of your vault and keep it somewhere private. The backup still requires your passphrase.

⌑

Change passphrase

Re-encrypt the vault with a new passphrase. Use at least 12 characters.

×

Delete this local vault

Delete the encrypted vault from this browser. This cannot be undone unless you have an encrypted backup.
